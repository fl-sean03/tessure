# Asset attribution

All site models, geometry, diagrams and world illustrations are made for this Tessure concept site. World geometry is reproducible from the corresponding scene module or included asset-generation source. Depicted places and events are fictional illustrations.

IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono are by IBM, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The self-hosted Latin WOFF2 files were obtained from the `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans` and `@fontsource/ibm-plex-mono` packages. License texts are in `public/fonts/`. Original project: https://github.com/IBM/plex.

No scraped imagery, remote environment maps, proprietary customer logos or external runtime asset services are used.

Social-preview artwork in `public/social/` combines these original world renders with the self-hosted IBM Plex typefaces. Each world page has its own illustrative preview.

The opening film and poster are rendered from the actual procedural logistics incident in `components/worlds/scenes/logistics-yard/`. The silent 14-second film follows one continuous camera route as the already suspended marked load A7 continues rising, travels past its empty assigned bay and lowers onto a waiting carrier. It samples authored time 12–26 at the scene's original pace, without cuts or time compression; these illustration times are not sensing or response performance. The 1152×1080 H.264 film uses 24 frames per second and absolute-time scene poses. Its WebP poster uses the same world at illustration time 12. In-world role annotations are omitted from the film to keep small-format type legible; accessible HTML captions describe the diversion and proposed use of site records, not real sensor feeds. The Camera, Radar and Thermal chips name proposed components modeled in the full world; this excerpt does not show each device or the later response. The interactive world contains the complete incident, narrated human review, staff intervention and operator-requested drone cycle. Geometry, texture grain and animation are generated locally; there is no audio or third-party footage.
